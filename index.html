      What I do best

      End-to-end UI design is where I live. Moodboard, wireframes, high-fidelity responsive UI, then a clean hand-off to developers.

      Flows and information architecture come before pixels. I map who does what, so big portals with many roles stay easy to move through.

      UX audits that find the friction. I look for the spots where users hesitate or give up, then redesign them, especially on mobile.
